Questions people ask

Can I get past the lock without answering?

Yes, deliberately. You can watch a short ad, or spend minutes you banked by practising earlier. A lock you can never get through stops being a tool and starts being a problem.

Does it read what I do in other apps?

No. Android's Usage Access tells QuizLock how long an app was in the foreground and nothing about what happened inside it. The names of the apps you monitor never leave your phone.

Why does it need "display over other apps"?

To draw the quiz over the app you just opened. Some phones — Xiaomi and relatives — also want their own "show pop-up windows while running in the background" permission, and QuizLock tells you if it catches that happening.

Will it drain my battery?

The monitor runs on its own background thread and samples usage periodically rather than continuously. It is built to be invisible in battery stats; if it is not on your phone, write to us.

I have a promo code.

Open Premium in the app and tap "Have a promo code?". Enter it there and the Play Store takes over; Premium switches on by itself once Play confirms the code.
